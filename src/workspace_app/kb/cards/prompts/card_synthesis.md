- **Lose nothing.** Every claim above must be represented. Two claims about the

  red."

  the statements above, however obvious it seems. What the corpus did not say is
  not part of this card; a reader who wanted general knowledge would not be
  looking here.

corpus's own vocabulary — if the statements say 回焊爐, the card says 回焊爐.
