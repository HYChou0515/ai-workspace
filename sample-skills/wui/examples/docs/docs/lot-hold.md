# When a lot is held

A held lot waits until someone decides. The decision is faster when the note
says what was seen, not what was guessed.

## What to write

- the lot and the step it stopped at;
- what the measurement showed, with the number;
- what you already ruled out.

## Who to tell

The shift lead first, then the process owner for that step.
